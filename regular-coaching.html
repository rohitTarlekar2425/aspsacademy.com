Description

ASP's Academy provides comprehensive Regular Coaching for students from 5th to 10th standard, covering all major subjects. Our coaching program focuses on building strong academic fundamentals, improving conceptual understanding, regular practice, and preparing students to perform confidently in school examinations.

Experienced faculty members provide personalized guidance, classroom learning, doubt-solving sessions, regular tests, revision, and exam-oriented preparation to help every student achieve better academic results.

Course Requirements

This course is designed for students studying in 5th to 10th standard. Students should attend regular classes, complete assigned practice, participate in tests, and actively work on their concepts.

  • Students from 5th to 10th standard can enroll.
  • Covers Mathematics, Science, English, Social Science and other school subjects.
  • Strong focus on fundamental concepts and academic improvement.
  • Regular homework, practice sessions and doubt-solving support.
  • Periodic tests and revision sessions for better exam preparation.
  • Personalized guidance to identify and improve weak areas.
